How the calls are made

  • Apply when Anant rates it A and GMP is at least 15% on the last morning, with institutions (QIB) showing up. Apply if… means it passes on GMP and rating but one check is still open. Wait means not open yet or not covered by Anant. Skip fails the gate.
  • Allotment odds are for one retail application. When retail is oversubscribed every applicant enters a lottery for exactly 1 lot, so odds are about 1 ÷ retail subscription and extra lots don't help. When it's undersubscribed you get everything you bid for.
  • Risk is the chance of losing money on listing day. Low: GMP 25%+ and steady, profitable, strong demand. Medium: GMP 15–25% or volatile, or one notable red flag. High: GMP under 15% or falling, loss-making, weak institutions, or SME.
  • Money assumes one PAN, 1 lot per IPO, and ₹3,00,000 total. SME IPOs only appear when Anant rates them A (left out this week: Shellz India, Ravita Engineering Services, Vaibhav Vyapaar).
  • Selling follows Anant's Rule of 15: for a good IPO, hold through the first 15 minutes and exit if the price breaks that 15-minute low. For an average one, sell in the pre-open.
